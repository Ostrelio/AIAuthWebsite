Privacy Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

What this policy covers

This policy covers the public Ostrelio website and its access-request form. It does not describe data processing inside a separately provided Ostrelio product or a customer’s systems.

Information we receive

When you request access, we receive the email address you submit. If you contact us, we also receive the information you include in your message. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information through the form.

Our hosting and delivery providers may process technical information such as your IP address, browser information, request times, and pages requested to deliver and protect the website.

How information is used

We use access requests to evaluate interest, respond to requests, and communicate about Ostrelio access. We use correspondence to answer questions and technical information to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the website.

Service providers and external resources

The access-request form sends your email to Formspree for processing and delivery. This website is hosted on Vercel. The homepage loads fonts from Google Fonts and OnlineWebFonts, icons through a CDN, and a background video through Amazon CloudFront. Loading those resources sends ordinary connection information, including your IP address, to their providers.

Providers handle information under their own terms and privacy notices. Information may be processed in countries other than your own. We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights, or prevent abuse.

Cookies and tracking

The website’s own code does not set cookies or use browser storage for advertising or analytics. External services may process technical information under their own policies. If our information-handling practices change, we will update this policy.

Retention and your choices

We retain information for the purposes described here and any applicable legal obligations. You may ask us to stop access-related messages or delete your access request by emailing rushilpunu@ostrelio.com. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information. We may need to verify a request before acting on it, and legal exceptions may apply.

Security and children

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. This website is intended for business users and is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has submitted personal information so we can address it.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy as the website changes. The date above identifies this version. For privacy questions or requests, contact rushilpunu@ostrelio.com.
